AEO: Answer Engine Optimization

People now get answers straight from Google snippets, voice assistants and AI tools, often without clicking a link. AEO makes sure your website is the source those answers come from.

Get an AEO plan

What is AEO?

AEO is the practice of writing and structuring your pages so search engines and AI assistants can pull a clear, correct answer from them. It works alongside normal SEO. Good rankings bring visitors, and clear answers get your brand quoted.

What I do

A focused set of changes that make your content easy to quote.

Question research

I find the real questions your customers ask in search, forums and "People also ask" boxes.

Answer-ready content

I rewrite or add short, direct answers near the top of each page, followed by supporting detail.

Structured data

I add FAQ, HowTo and other schema where it fits, so search engines understand each page.

  • Featured snippet and "People also ask" targeting
  • Voice search friendly phrasing
  • FAQ sections and clear headings
  • Entity and brand clarity across your site

How I work

A simple process, so you always know what comes next.

  1. Audit

    I check which of your pages already answer questions and where competitors win the snippet.

  2. Plan

    You get a short list of questions and pages to work on first.

  3. Optimise

    I rewrite answers, fix headings and add structured data.

  4. Track

    I monitor snippets, impressions and AI mentions, then adjust.

No one can promise a snippet or an AI mention. What I can do is remove what holds your content back and make it the clearest answer on the page.
