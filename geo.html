What is GEO?

Generative AI tools build answers from sources they find trustworthy and easy to understand. GEO improves how clear, credible and visible your brand is across the web, so those tools pick you as a source. It builds on solid SEO rather than replacing it.

What I do

Practical work on both your site and your wider online presence.

Citable content

Brand and entity signals

How I work

A simple process, so you always know what comes next.

  1. Check

    I review how AI tools describe your brand and which sources they use.

  2. Plan

  3. Optimise

  4. Monitor

    I re-test key questions on a schedule and adjust the plan.
